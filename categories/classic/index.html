About unblocked classic games

Classic games earned their status for a reason: the rules fit on a napkin, but the skill ceiling does not. This collection rebuilds the icons of the arcade and early home-computer era as clean, modern HTML5 ports that work on a Chromebook with nothing installed.

No emulator, no ROM download, no shady mirror site. Just the game, a keyboard or touchscreen, and a local high-score table that remembers your best run.

Every game in this category has its own page with a controls table, a short guide and a stored high score. You can move between titles without leaving the site, and the full catalogue is one click away.

Classic Games FAQ

What makes a game a classic?

A rule set simple enough to explain in one sentence and deep enough to keep playing: Snake, Tetris, Pac-Man, Pong, Space Invaders, Chess, Connect Four and Tic-Tac-Toe.

Are these the original versions?

They are original HTML5 recreations, not emulated ROMs. The rules and feel are faithful; the code and artwork are new.

Do the classic games work offline?

The pages are static and cached by the browser. Once loaded, the game code itself needs no network connection.
