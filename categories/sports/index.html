About unblocked sports games

Not every sports game needs a controller, a roster update, or a forty-hour career mode. Sometimes you want one rally, one save, one perfectly placed shot. Pong, Air Hockey and Penalty Shootout are built for exactly that kind of break.

Both games support a second player on the same keyboard where it makes sense, and both work with touch. Matches are short by design, so you can finish a round even when the bell is about to ring.

Every game in this category has its own page with a controls table, a short guide and a stored high score. You can move between titles without leaving the site, and the full catalogue is one click away.

Sports Games FAQ

Which sports games can I play?

Pong and Air Hockey support single-player against the computer or two players on one keyboard. Penalty Shootout is a five-round football shootout with a diving keeper.

Can two people play on the same device?

Pong has a two-player mode: W/S controls the left paddle and the arrow keys control the right. Penalty Shootout is single-player against the computer.

Are the sports games realistic simulations?

No. They are compact arcade versions of the sport, designed for a two-minute break rather than a full season mode.
