About unblocked idle & clicker games

Idle games are the perfect companion to a busy screen. Click a few times, buy your first upgrade, and the numbers start climbing on their own. Cookie Clicker distills that loop into a single page: bake cookies, buy producers, unlock milestones, and come back to a bigger empire.

Your bakery is saved to localStorage every few seconds, so it survives refreshes and tab switches. There is no account and no cloud requirement, which also makes it work on restricted school networks.

Every game in this category has its own page with a controls table, a short guide and a stored high score. You can move between titles without leaving the site, and the full catalogue is one click away.

Idle & Clicker Games FAQ

What is an idle game?

A game that keeps producing resources while you are not actively clicking. Cookie Clicker is the genre-defining example: buy producers, close the tab, come back to more cookies.

Does Cookie Clicker progress while the tab is closed?

Yes, at a reduced offline rate. The game records the save time and credits up to four hours of offline production when you return.

Is Cookie Clicker free?

Yes. There are no microtransactions, no premium currency and no account. Your bakery is saved in local storage on your device.
