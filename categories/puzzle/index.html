About unblocked puzzle games

Puzzle games are the quiet champions of the school-break genre. They load in a second, ask for nothing but your attention, and reward the kind of careful thinking that a five-minute break can actually accommodate. Our unblocked puzzle collection is built around that idea: short sessions, deep play, and no download in sight.

Every puzzle here runs entirely in the browser on HTML5 canvas, so there is no plugin to install and no launcher to fight with. Progress and best scores are saved locally in your browser through localStorage, which means your high score survives a page refresh without ever creating an account.

Every game in this category has its own page with a controls table, a short guide and a stored high score. You can move between titles without leaving the site, and the full catalogue is one click away.

Puzzle Games FAQ

What counts as a puzzle game?

Games where the challenge is logic, planning or memory rather than reflexes: 2048, Tetris, Sudoku, Minesweeper, Memory Match and Chess.

Which puzzle game is best for a short break?

2048 and Memory Match can be played in a few minutes. Sudoku and Minesweeper are better when you have a longer stretch of time.

Do puzzle games save progress?

Yes. 2048 saves the current board, Minesweeper saves best times per difficulty, Sudoku saves the current puzzle and Memory Match saves best move counts.
