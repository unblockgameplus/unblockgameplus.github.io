About unblocked arcade games

Arcade games are designed around a single perfect loop: learn the rule in three seconds, lose in thirty, and immediately want to try again. This collection keeps that loop intact while removing the things that make old arcade pages painful — pop-ups, plugin prompts, and thirty-second loading screens.

Each game starts from a tap or a key press and restarts instantly. Scores are stored on your own device, so you can chase a personal best across a whole class period without leaving a trail of accounts behind.

Every game in this category has its own page with a controls table, a short guide and a stored high score. You can move between titles without leaving the site, and the full catalogue is one click away.

Arcade Games FAQ

What is an arcade game?

A game built around short, repeatable runs and a score counter: Snake, Pac-Man, Breakout, Flappy Bird, Sky Hopper and Stack Master all fit that description.

Why do arcade games restart so quickly?

The restart loop is the point. Every game here restarts with a single button press or the R key, so a failed run costs seconds rather than a loading screen.

Are the arcade games playable on touch screens?

Yes. Swipe gestures and on-screen directional pads are provided where a game needs them.
