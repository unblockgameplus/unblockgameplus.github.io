About unblocked 2 player games

Two-player games are the fastest way to turn a break into a competition. Every game in this category can be played by two people on the same device: one keyboard, one trackpad, or two halves of a touchscreen. No lobby, no account, no waiting for a match.

The collection covers different speeds of play: Pong and Air Hockey are reflex duels, Tic-Tac-Toe is a quick logic round, and Connect Four is a deeper strategy game that rewards planning several moves ahead. Each game also includes a computer opponent if you are playing solo.

Every game in this category has its own page with a controls table, a short guide and a stored high score. You can move between titles without leaving the site, and the full catalogue is one click away.

2 Player Games FAQ

Which games can two people play on one device?

Pong, Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four and Air Hockey all have a local two-player mode. You share one keyboard or touchscreen, with no lobby or second account.

Can we play two-player games on a phone?

Yes. Air Hockey supports dragging on each half of the table, and Connect Four and Tic-Tac-Toe work with taps. Pong is best with a keyboard but works with touch on larger screens.

Do the two-player games work against a computer too?

Yes. Connect Four, Pong and Air Hockey include computer opponents, so you can play solo and then switch to two-player mode when a friend joins.
