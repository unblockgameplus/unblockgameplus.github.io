About Unblocked Games G+

A clean, fast browser game portal with built-in local fallbacks, built for school Chromebooks and short breaks.

Why this site exists

Most "unblocked games" websites are cluttered directories loaded with heavy ads that embed games from unverified third-party domains. When those embeds are filtered or taken down, the page breaks. Unblocked Games G+ takes a hybrid approach: a large 800+ curated third-party catalogue loads automatically in a clean wrapper, supported by 26 original built-in games hosted directly on this domain as reliable offline fallbacks.

How the games are built

Our local fallback games are custom HTML5 implementations using the 2D canvas API, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. They require zero plugins, no Flash, and no third-party runtimes. Game progress and high scores are saved locally in your browser through localStorage without requiring account registration.

No account required & device storage

We respect your privacy: high scores, settings, and progress remain on your device. We do not require signups, passwords, or personal identity submissions.

Contributing and Feedback

We welcome feedback, game requests, and bug reports. Please visit our contact page or submit an issue on our GitHub repository.
