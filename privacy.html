Privacy Policy

What this site stores, what it does not, and how to clear it.

Last updated: 2026-10-19.

Information we do not collect

Unblocked Games G+ does not require an account, an email address or a payment method. We do not ask for your name, school, age or location. There is no login system and no user-generated content.

Local storage

The games use your browser's localStorage to remember things like high scores and the current 2048 board. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser's site-data settings, or by using the reset button inside a game.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-8JCKVMCC4Z) and Microsoft Clarity (project ID z062bfytj7) to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site. These services may set cookies and collect standard technical information such as page URL, referrer, approximate region, device type and browser. IP addresses are anonymised in the analytics configuration. Their handling of data is governed by Google's and Microsoft's respective privacy policies.

Advertising and third-party game embeds

Some game pages load a third-party HTML5 game in an iframe after you press Play. The game host may receive standard request data (IP address, browser, referrer) and may set its own cookies or show its own advertising. We do not control the third-party host and do not inject its original wrapper’s ad or tracking scripts. Check the host’s own privacy policy if you need details.

Our local fallback games are served from this domain and do not contact a third-party game host.

Children

The site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because there are no accounts or forms that submit personal data, no such information is stored on our side.

Your choices

You can block analytics with a content blocker or your browser's tracking-protection setting without affecting gameplay. Clearing site data removes saved scores and progress; it does not affect your ability to play.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page or opened as an issue on GitHub.
